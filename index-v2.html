How it works

Three steps, and two of them are ours.

You send photos. We do the rest. Nothing to learn, no dashboard, no software to sign up for.

1

Tell us about the business

Fill in the form, or reply to our email. Your logo if you have one, a few photos, your hours and phone number. That's genuinely all we need — we write the words.

2

We build it and send you a link

About a week. You look at the real thing on your own phone and tell us what to change. We change it. There's no limit on that part.

3

It goes live on your own domain

We register the domain, point it, and turn it on. You own it. If you ever leave us, it goes with you.
