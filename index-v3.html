The honest version

Most local businesses are running on a Facebook page and a phone number.

That worked for a long time. It stopped working when people started deciding who to call from a phone in a parking lot at 8pm.

What that costs you

  • — Someone searches your trade at 9pm. You don't come up. Your competitor does.

  • — No way to book you without talking to you first.
  • — On a phone, the page pinches and zooms. People leave.

How this works

We build it first. You pay if you like it.

You have never met us and we turned up in your messages. So we are not going to ask you for money before you have seen the thing. Here is the whole process, start to finish.

You say yes

One line back to us — your business name and roughly what you do. That is the entire commitment. No form, no call, no card, nothing to sign.

We build your actual site

Not a template with your name dropped in. Your services, your hours, your prices, your photos if you send them and stock ones if you don't. Two or three days. You do nothing while we do it.

You look at it

We send you a link only you have. Open it on your phone at the counter. Anything wrong — wrong hours, wrong prices, hate the colour — tell us and we fix it. Twice, three times, however many times it takes.

You decide

Like it: $500 and it goes live on your own domain, usually the same day. Don't: close the tab. No invoice, no follow-up, nothing owed, and we won't message you again. We wear the work.

That last part is not generosity. It is the only honest way to prove we are real to somebody who has never met us — and if we can't build something you'd happily pay for, we shouldn't get paid.
